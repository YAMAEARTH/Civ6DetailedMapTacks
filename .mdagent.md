## 1. Project Objectives & Overview

The standard **Detailed Map Tacks** mod calculates adjacency bonuses and placement validity for map pins placed manually by the player. This enhanced version extends DMT with two major automated planning features:

1. **Automatic Settler City Placement Recommendation & Top 3 Scoring**:
   - When any unit with city-founding capability (e.g., `UNIT_SETTLER`) is selected or finishes moving, the game analyzes all valid tiles in the surrounding area.
   - Evaluates water availability (fresh water/coast), city center tile yields, ring 1 & ring 2 resource yields, terrain defense, movement distance penalty, and Firaxis Strategic AI suggestions.
   - Automatically places Top 3 City Center map tacks on the map with their rank and exact score (e.g. `#1 Settle (Score: 84) [Fresh Water]`).
2. **Automated Specialty District Adjacency & Boost Optimizer (All 16 Districts)**:
   - When a city is founded (`Events.CityAddedToMap`) or triggered via `Shift + A`, all previous settler recommendation pins are cleaned up.
   - The city's workable 3-ring radius is evaluated to find the highest-yielding, conflict-free layout for all **16 specialty districts and infrastructure**:
     - **Aqueduct** (`DISTRICT_AQUEDUCT` / Bath) (infrastructure hub connecting to river/mountain/lake/oasis)
     - **Dam** (`DISTRICT_DAM`) (floodplain protection and major IZ adjacency)
     - **Canal** (`DISTRICT_CANAL`) (flat land navigation and +2 IZ adjacency)
     - **Industrial Zone** (`DISTRICT_INDUSTRIAL_ZONE` / Hansa / Oppidum) (maximizing Aqueduct, Dam, Canal, strategics, and mines)
     - **Commercial Hub** (`DISTRICT_COMMERCIAL_HUB` / Suguba) & **Harbor** (`DISTRICT_HARBOR` / Royal Navy Dockyard / Cothon) (river and coastal trade synergy)
     - **Campus** (`DISTRICT_CAMPUS` / Seowon) (mountains, geothermal fissures, reefs, rainforests)
     - **Holy Site** (`DISTRICT_HOLY_SITE` / Lavra) (mountains, natural wonders, woods)
     - **Entertainment Complex** (`DISTRICT_ENTERTAINMENT_COMPLEX` / Street Carnival) (+1 Amenity and +2 major adjacency to Theater Square)
     - **Theater Square** (`DISTRICT_THEATER` / Acropolis) (Entertainment Complex, wonders, district clusters)
     - **Government Plaza** (`DISTRICT_GOVERNMENT`) (1 per empire, capital prioritized, central hub boosting all adjacent districts)
     - **Diplomatic Quarter** (`DISTRICT_DIPLOMATIC_QUARTER`) (1 per empire, central diplomatic hub, boosts adjacent districts & envoys)
     - **Encampment** (`DISTRICT_ENCAMPMENT` / Thành / Ikanda) (strictly placed $\ge 2$ hexes away from City Center, favoring high defense hills and perimeter)
     - **Neighborhood** (`DISTRICT_NEIGHBORHOOD` / Mbanza) (optimizes for highest Appeal $\ge 2$ providing up to +6 Housing)
     - **Aerodrome** (`DISTRICT_AERODROME`) (flat land placement, 4 air unit capacity)
     - **Spaceport** (`DISTRICT_SPACEPORT`) (flat land placement, science victory projects)
   - **City Identification & Tagging**:
     - Automatically distinguishes capital vs secondary cities: `[เมืองหลัก: Washington]` vs `[เมืองรอง: New York]`.
     - Pins are labeled with full city context, e.g. `[เมืองหลัก: Washington] Campus (+4 [ICON_Science] Sci)`.
   - **Turn-by-Turn Dynamic Border & District Verification**:
     - Every turn (`Events.LocalPlayerTurnBegin`, `Events.PlayerTurnActivated`) and on savegame load (`Events.LoadGameViewStateDone`), scans all placed district pins.
     - Automatically removes pins if the tile was occupied by foreign border expansion, if a district was constructed, or if the city was razed/lost.
     - Automatically re-plans optimal layouts for cities that lost tiles to ensure continuous efficiency.

## 2. Client-Side Only & Multiplayer Safety Architecture

A primary requirement for this mod is that **friends in multiplayer games do not need to install the mod**, and it will **never cause desynchronization (desync)** or require host approval.

### How this is achieved:
1. **Zero Database Alterations**:
   - The mod does not modify core gameplay tables (`GameInfo`, units, buildings, districts database values) on the server.
   - It runs strictly as an InGame UI script (`<AddUserInterfaces Context="InGame">`).
2. **Safe Saved Game Tag**:
   - Configured with `<AffectsSavedGames>0</AffectsSavedGames>` in `DetailedMapTacks.modinfo`. Saves can be loaded or shared freely without requiring the mod.
3. **Private Map Pin Visibility**:
   - All auto-placed pins are assigned visibility `pin:SetVisibility(localPlayerID)`.
   - In Civ 6 multiplayer, a positive player ID specifies that the pin is **private** to that player (`PlayerTargetTypes.CHATTARGET_PLAYER`). Other human players on the server do not receive these pins in their UI and cannot experience desyncs.

## 3. Algorithms & Logic

### 3.1 Settler Recommendation Algorithm (`RecommendSettlerSpots`)

#### Candidate Plot Filtering:
- Must be explored/revealed to the local player (`pPlot:IsRevealed(playerID)`).
- Must be valid land (`not pPlot:IsWater()`, `not pPlot:IsMountain()`, `not pPlot:IsImpassable()`).
- Must not be inside closed foreign territory (`pPlot:IsOwned() and pPlot:GetOwner() ~= playerID`).
- **City Proximity Check**: Must be strictly farther than 3 tiles (`CITY_MIN_RANGE`) from **all** existing city centers across all known players.

#### Scoring Formula:
$$\text{Total Score} = S_{\text{water}} + S_{\text{base}} + S_{\text{ring1}} + S_{\text{ring2}} - P_{\text{distance}} + B_{\text{AI}}$$

1. **Water & Housing ($S_{\text{water}}$)**:
   - Fresh Water (`plot:IsFreshWater()` - River, Lake, Oasis): **+26 pts** (Starts with 5 Housing).
   - Coastal Land (`plot:IsCoastalLand()`): **+12 pts** (Starts with 3 Housing).
   - No Water: **+0 pts** (Starts with 2 Housing - heavy growth penalty).
2. **City Center Tile Base ($S_{\text{base}}$)**:
   - Plains Hills: **+12 pts** (Free 2 Food / 2 Production tile base + 3 combat defense strength).
   - Other Hills: **+5 pts** (+3 combat defense strength).
   - Luxury Resource on tile: **+8 pts** (Immediate amenity hookup, immune to pillaging).
   - Strategic Resource on tile: **+6 pts** (Immediate resource accumulation once researched).
3. **Ring 1 Workable Yields ($S_{\text{ring1}}$ - 6 adjacent tiles)**:
   - Food: $\ge 3$ Food: **+5 pts** (Fast growth to population 2), otherwise $\text{Food} \times 1.5$.
   - Production: $\ge 2$ Production: **+6 pts** (Accelerated first unit/monument), otherwise $\text{Prod} \times 2.0$.
   - Science / Culture: $\times 2.5$ pts per yield.
   - Faith: $\times 2.0$ pts per yield.
   - Gold: $\times 0.5$ pts per yield.
   - Luxury Resource: **+7 pts**.
   - Strategic Resource: **+5 pts**.
   - Bonus Resource (Wheat, Cattle, Rice, Deer, etc.): **+3 pts**.
   - Choppable Feature (Woods, Rainforest, Marsh, Stone): **+2 pts**.
   - Geothermal Fissure / Reef: **+4 pts** (Campus setup).
   - Mountain: **+2.5 pts** (Adjacency setup).
4. **Ring 2 Workable Yields ($S_{\text{ring2}}$ - 12 tiles in radius 2)**:
   - Food: $\times 0.8$ pts.
   - Production: $\times 1.0$ pts.
   - Luxury: **+4 pts**, Strategic: **+3 pts**.
   - Natural Wonder: **+10 pts**.
5. **Movement Distance Penalty ($P_{\text{distance}}$)**:
   - 0 moves (settle in place): **0 penalty**.
   - 1 hex away: **-2 pts**.
   - 2 hexes away: **-5 pts**.
   - 3 hexes away: **-9 pts**.
   - $> 3$ hexes away: $-9 - ((\text{dist} - 3) \times 4)$ pts.
6. **Firaxis Grand Strategic AI Bonus ($B_{\text{AI}}$)**:
   - If Firaxis's `pGrandAI:GetSettlementRecommendations` also chose this tile: **+10 pts**.

#### Pin Placement:
- Top 3 scored plots receive Map Pins with icon `ICON_DISTRICT_CITY_CENTER` and labels:
  - `#1 Settle (Score: XX) [Fresh Water]`
  - `#2 Settle (Score: XX) ...`
  - `#3 Settle (Score: XX) ...`

#### 3.2 City District Boost Optimizer (`OptimizeCityDistricts`)

When a city is settled, or when `Shift + A` is pressed, the optimizer performs a sequenced constraint-satisfaction assignment across all 16 specialty districts:

```
[City Detected: Capital / Secondary] 
      │
      ├─► Clear Previous Auto-Pins for This City
      │
      ├─► Step A: Place Aqueduct (City Center + River/Lake/Mountain)
      ├─► Step B: Place Dam (Floodplains with >= 2 river edges)
      ├─► Step C: Place Canal (Flat land connecting water bodies / city center)
      ├─► Step D: Place Industrial Zone (Adjacent to Aqueduct + Dam + Canal + Strategics + Mines)
      ├─► Step E: Place Harbor (Coast water + City Center / Sea resources)
      ├─► Step F: Place Commercial Hub (River + Harbor + District cluster)
      ├─► Step G: Place Campus (Mountains + Geothermal + Reefs + Rainforests)
      ├─► Step H: Place Holy Site (Mountains + Natural Wonders + Woods)
      ├─► Step I: Place Entertainment Complex (Amenity hub + neighbor for Theater Square)
      ├─► Step J: Place Theater Square (+2 from Entertainment Complex + Wonders + Clusters)
      ├─► Step K: Place Government Plaza (1/empire, Capital priority, central +1 boost hub)
      ├─► Step L: Place Diplomatic Quarter (1/empire, Envoy boost + district cluster)
      ├─► Step M: Place Encampment (Strictly >= 2 hexes from City Center, Hills/defense)
      ├─► Step N: Place Neighborhood (Highest Appeal >= 2, up to +6 Housing)
      ├─► Step O: Place Aerodrome (Flat land, outer ring, 4 air slots)
      └─► Step P: Place Spaceport (Flat land, outer ring, Science Victory projects)
```

1. **City Tagging & Ownership**:
   - Each pin name is tagged with `[เมืองหลัก: CityName]` (Capital) or `[เมืองรอง: CityName]` (Secondary).
   - Prevents mixing of pins between neighboring cities.
2. **Preservation of Manual Pins**:
   - Any genuine pin manually placed by the player is respected and will never be overwritten.
3. **Unique District Mapping**:
   - Checks player civilization traits to substitute unique districts automatically:
     - `DISTRICT_INDUSTRIAL_ZONE` $\to$ `DISTRICT_HANSA` (Germany) / `DISTRICT_OPPIDUM` (Gaul)
     - `DISTRICT_CAMPUS` $\to$ `DISTRICT_SEOWON` (Korea) / `DISTRICT_OBSERVATORY` (Maya)
     - `DISTRICT_COMMERCIAL_HUB` $\to$ `DISTRICT_SUGUBA` (Mali)
     - `DISTRICT_AQUEDUCT` $\to$ `DISTRICT_BATH` (Rome)
     - `DISTRICT_HOLY_SITE` $\to$ `DISTRICT_LAVRA` (Russia)
     - `DISTRICT_HARBOR` $\to$ `DISTRICT_ROYAL_NAVY_DOCKYARD` (England) / `DISTRICT_COTHON` (Phoenicia)
     - `DISTRICT_THEATER` $\to$ `DISTRICT_ACROPOLIS` (Greece)
     - `DISTRICT_ENCAMPMENT` $\to$ `DISTRICT_THANH` (Vietnam) / `DISTRICT_IKANDA` (Zulu)
     - `DISTRICT_NEIGHBORHOOD` $\to$ `DISTRICT_MBANZA` (Kongo)
     - `DISTRICT_ENTERTAINMENT_COMPLEX` $\to$ `DISTRICT_STREET_CARNIVAL` (Brazil)
4. **Collision Avoidance**:
   - Tracks assigned plots so that no two districts are ever planned on the same hex.
5. **Detailed Map Tacks Yield Broadcast**:
   - Upon creating the pins, `Network.BroadcastPlayerInfo()` and `LuaEvents.DMT_MapPinAdded` are triggered, causing DMT to immediately compute and render the exact yield badges over the pins on screen!

### 3.3 Turn-by-Turn Dynamic Border & District Validation (`ValidateAndRefreshAutoPins`)

- **Execution Triggers**:
  - `Events.LocalPlayerTurnBegin`: At the beginning of each turn.
  - `Events.PlayerTurnActivated`: When player controls become active.
  - `Events.LoadGameViewStateDone`: Upon loading a savegame.
- **Validation Checks**:
  1. **Foreign Border Expansion / Conquest**: If an opponent civ claims a tile previously reserved for a district (`plot:IsOwned() and plot:GetOwner() ~= playerID`), the tack is immediately deleted and the owning city is flagged for automatic re-planning.
  2. **District Already Built**: If a district or wonder is constructed on that plot (`plot:GetDistrictType() ~= -1 or plot:IsCity()`), the tack is cleared cleanly.
  3. **City Lost or Razed**: If the owning city no longer belongs to the player, all its auto-pins are deleted.
- **Automatic Re-planning**:
  - For any city whose district was blocked or lost to border shifts, `OptimizeCityDistricts` runs automatically to re-route remaining districts to new optimal plots.

## 5. Usage & Verification Guide

### In-Game Usage & Controls:
1. **Shortcut Key `Shift + A` (Smart Planner Hotkey)**:
   - Press **`Shift + A`** at any time during your turn:
     - **If a Settler is selected**: Instantly calculates and marks the Top 3 Settler spots on the map with `#1`, `#2`, `#3` and displays the floating HUD Settler Recommendation panel.
     - **If a City is selected**: Instantly calculates and pins the optimal Specialty District plan for that city and opens the City District Planning HUD panel.
     - **If the mouse cursor is over any tile of your City**: Automatically detects the owning city, selects it, and optimizes its districts!
     - **If any Settler is available in your empire**: Focuses camera on the Settler, selects it, and displays the Top 3 recommendations.
     - **Fallback**: Focuses on your Capital and runs the district boost optimizer.
2. **Automatic Triggers**:
   - Selecting a Settler or moving a Settler displays the Top 3 Settler recommendations and the HUD panel.
   - Founding a new city (`Events.CityAddedToMap`) automatically clears Settler pins and calculates the optimal district layout.
3. **Floating HUD UI Panels & Close Controls**:
   - **Settler Recommendation Panel** (Top-Left): Shows ranking cards (`#1`, `#2`, `#3`), total scores, fresh water/coastal housing tags, terrain details, and a "ดูตำแหน่ง" (Look At) camera button.
   - **City District Plan Panel** (Top-Center): Shows each planned specialty district with its official game icon (`ICON_DISTRICT_*`), district name, projected adjacency yield bonus, and a "ส่องดูผังเมือง" camera button.
   - **Closing Panels**:
     - **Close Button [X]**: Click the close button on the top-right corner of the window (complete with mouse-over hover and click audio feedback).
     - **ESC Key**: Press **`ESC`** to instantly close whichever HUD panel is open without deselecting your units.
     - **Dismissal Persistence**: Once dismissed by the player, the panel stays closed while you move your Settler around, preventing annoying repetitive popups. Press **`Shift + A`** whenever you want to reopen it!
4. **Map Pins & DMT Integration**:
   - City Center recommendations: Pinned with `ICON_DISTRICT_CITY_CENTER` and title `#1 ตั้งเมือง [XX คะแนน]`.
   - Specialty districts: Pinned with exact icons matching DetailedMapTacks (`ICON_DISTRICT_CAMPUS`, `ICON_DISTRICT_INDUSTRIAL_ZONE`, `ICON_DISTRICT_ENCAMPMENT`, etc.), prefixed with city context: `[เมืองหลัก: Washington] Campus (+4 [ICON_Science] Sci)`, immediately triggering DMT's real-time adjacency yield badges over each map tack.
   - Dynamic Turn Validation ensures all pins stay accurate, clearing obsolete/conquered tiles and re-planning dynamically.
