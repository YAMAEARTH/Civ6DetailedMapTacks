# DMT Smart Planner (.mdagent.md)

Detailed documentation and architectural guide for **Civ6DetailedMapTacks (Smart Planner Edition)**.

- **Repository**: [YAMAEARTH/Civ6DetailedMapTacks](https://github.com/YAMAEARTH/Civ6DetailedMapTacks.git)

## 1. Project Objectives & Overview

The standard **Detailed Map Tacks** mod calculates adjacency bonuses and placement validity for map pins placed manually by the player. This enhanced version extends DMT with two major automated planning features:

1. **Automatic Settler City Placement Recommendation & Top 3 Scoring**:
   - When any unit with city-founding capability (e.g., `UNIT_SETTLER`) is selected or finishes moving, the game analyzes all valid tiles in the surrounding area.
   - Evaluates water availability (fresh water/coast), city center tile yields, ring 1 & ring 2 resource yields, terrain defense, movement distance penalty, and Firaxis Strategic AI suggestions.
   - Automatically places Top 3 City Center map tacks on the map with their rank and exact score (e.g. `#1 Settle (Score: 84) [Fresh Water]`).
2. **Automated Specialty District Adjacency & Boost Optimizer (All 16 Districts) with Full Engine Mechanics**:
   - When a city is founded (`Events.CityAddedToMap`) or triggered via `Shift + A`, all previous settler recommendation pins are cleaned up.
   - The city's workable 3-ring radius is evaluated with strict adherence to Civ 6 engine constraints:
     - **Dam 1-per-River & River Floodplain Rule**: Validates that no other city or pin on the same river system has a Dam (`IsDamAlreadyOnRiver`) and strictly forbids placing Dams on Coastal Floodplains (`TerrainManager.GetCoastalLowlandType`). Requires true River Floodplains (`FEATURE_FLOODPLAINS_GRASSLAND`, `FEATURE_FLOODPLAINS_PLAINS`, `FEATURE_FLOODPLAINS`).
     - **Aqueduct / River Bridge Conditional Planning & Boost Optimization**:
       - *Engine Placement Constraint*: By Civ 6 engine rules (`IsAdjacentToCityCenter`), Aqueducts (สะพานส่งน้ำ) MUST be built adjacent to the City Center (Ring 1) and adjacent to a River/Lake/Oasis/Mountain. They cannot be placed far away (Ring 2 or 3).
       - *Smart Need & Boost Evaluation*: Because it consumes a valuable tile adjacent to the City Center, it is ONLY marked if it provides a genuine boost:
         1. *Fresh Water Boost*: If the city has NO fresh water (`not cityHasFreshWater`, e.g. Coastal or Dry land), it boosts Housing from 2–3 to 6 (`+4 Housing (น้ำจืด)`). Essential!
         2. *Rome Unique District*: Rome's Bath provides +1 Amenity and +2 Housing at half cost (`+2 Housing, +1 Amenity`).
         3. *Industrial Zone Adjacency Boost*: If the city ALREADY has fresh water, an Aqueduct is ONLY marked if it directly pairs with an adjacent Industrial Zone candidate achieving $\ge +3$ Production (`+2 Housing • บัฟโรงงาน +2`).
         4. *Skip when Unnecessary*: If the city already has fresh water, is not Rome, and no $\ge +3$ Production Industrial Zone synergy exists, the Aqueduct is **completely skipped**, saving the prime City-Center-adjacent tile for Campus, Commercial Hub, Holy Site, or Theater Square!
     - **Resource Protection & Harvest Tech Requirement**: Absolute ban on placing districts over Luxury resources, and over Strategic resources revealed by researched technologies. For Bonus Resources (Stone, Wheat, Cattle, Deer, etc.), placement is strictly blocked until the player has researched the requisite harvest technology (Masonry, Pottery, Animal Husbandry, Mining, Irrigation).
     - **Canal Valid Geometry & 60-Degree Turn Rule**: Must be flat land connecting 2 water bodies or 1 water body + 1 City Center. The two connected endpoints must not be adjacent to each other ($\text{distance} \ge 2$) to prevent illegal sharp bends ($\le 60^\circ$).
     - **Workable Range Strictness**: Strictly limited to Rings 1–3 (`1 <= Distance <= 3`).
     - **Civilization-Specific Unique District Rules**:
       - *Korea (Seowon)*: Must be on Hills, isolated from City Center and all other districts (distance $\ge 2$, 0 adjacent districts) to prevent the -1 Science penalty. Subsequent districts are forbidden from touching the Seowon.
       - *Kongo (Mbanza)*: Must be on Woods or Rainforest only, ignoring Appeal.
       - *Vietnam (Thành & all land specialty districts)*: Must be placed on Woods, Rainforest, or Marsh only.
       - *Gaul (Oppidum)*: Must not be adjacent to City Center (distance $\ge 2$).
     - **Population Cap & Build Priority Ranking**:
       - **Non-Specialty Districts**: Aqueduct, Bath, Dam, Canal, Neighborhood, Mbanza, Spaceport, Government Plaza, and Diplomatic Quarter do NOT consume population slots (built for free / "ไม่จำกัด Pop").
       - **Specialty Districts**: Aerodrome, Campus, Industrial Zone, Commercial Hub, Harbor, Holy Site, Theater Square, Encampment, Entertainment Complex strictly count toward the population limit (Pop 1 for 1st, Pop 4 for 2nd, Pop 7 for 3rd, etc., with Germany gaining +1 district bonus).
       - Automatically calculates strategic build priority (`#1`, `#2`, `#3`...) based on game phase, adjacency yields, and population thresholds.
   - **City Identification & Pin Naming**:
     - Places City Name directly at the front with build priority rank: `[CityName] #Rank DistrictName (Yield)` (e.g. `[Washington] #1 Campus (+4 [ICON_Science] Sci)`).
   - **Turn-by-Turn Dynamic Border & District Verification**:
     - Every turn (`Events.LocalPlayerTurnBegin`, `Events.PlayerTurnActivated`) and on savegame load (`Events.LoadGameViewStateDone`), scans all placed district pins.
     - Automatically removes pins if the tile was occupied by foreign border expansion, if a district was constructed, or if the city was razed/lost.
     - Automatically re-plans optimal layouts for cities that lost tiles to ensure continuous efficiency.

## 2. Client-Side Only & Multiplayer Safety Architecture

A primary requirement for this mod is that **friends in multiplayer games do not need to install the mod**, and it will **never cause desynchronization (desync)** or require host approval.

### How this is achieved:
1. **Zero Database Alterations**:
   - The mod does not modify core gameplay tables (`GameInfo`, units, buildings, districts database values) on the server.
   - It runs strictly as an InGame UI script (`<AddUserInterfaces Context="InGame">`).
2. **Safe Saved Game Tag**:
   - Configured with `<AffectsSavedGames>0</AffectsSavedGames>` in `DetailedMapTacks.modinfo`. Saves can be loaded or shared freely without requiring the mod.
3. **Private Map Pin Visibility**:
   - All auto-placed pins are assigned visibility `pin:SetVisibility(localPlayerID)`.
   - In Civ 6 multiplayer, a positive player ID specifies that the pin is **private** to that player (`PlayerTargetTypes.CHATTARGET_PLAYER`). Other human players on the server do not receive these pins in their UI and cannot experience desyncs.

## 3. Algorithms & Logic

### 3.1 Settler Recommendation Algorithm (`RecommendSettlerSpots`)

#### Candidate Plot Filtering & Search Anchor:
- **Search Space Anchoring**:
  - When $\text{cityCount} \ge 1$: Scans rings 4 to 7 around **all** existing friendly City Centers to guarantee contiguous AoE and district synergy connections, plus tiles within 4 hexes of the Settler unit and Grand AI recommendations.
  - When $\text{cityCount} == 0$ (Turn 1 / Initial Settler): Scans radius 5 hexes around the Settler unit.
- Must be explored/revealed to the local player (`pPlot:IsRevealed(playerID)`).
- Must be valid land (`not pPlot:IsWater()`, `not pPlot:IsMountain()`, `not pPlot:IsImpassable()`).
- Must not be inside closed foreign territory (`pPlot:IsOwned() and pPlot:GetOwner() ~= playerID`).
- **City Proximity Check**: Must be strictly farther than 3 tiles (`CITY_MIN_RANGE`, distance $\ge 4$) from **all** existing city centers across all known players.
- **Score Rejection Filter**: Any plot resulting in $\text{score} \le -9000$ (due to critical loyalty or illegal proximity) is strictly discarded from the recommendation pool.

#### Scoring Formula:
$$\text{Total Score} = S_{\text{loyalty}} + S_{\text{water}} + S_{\text{base}} + S_{\text{ring1}} + S_{\text{ring2}} - P_{\text{distance}} + B_{\text{AI}} + S_{\text{city\_dist}}$$

0. **Loyalty Pressure ($S_{\text{loyalty}}$ - Gathering Storm / Rise & Fall)**:
   - Uses Firaxis engine `Map.GetContinentPlotsLoyalty()` to read per-plot loyalty pressure per turn:
   - $\text{Loyalty} \le -10$: **Critical Loyalty Risk!** Returns **-9999 pts** immediately ("แรงกดดัน Loyalty วิกฤต เสี่ยงเมืองแตกเป็น Free City") and is completely blocked from candidate recommendations.
   - $-10 < \text{Loyalty} < 0$: Moderate foreign pressure, penalizes score heavily by $\text{Loyalty} \times 2.5$ pts (e.g. -6 pressure = -15 pts).
   - $\text{Loyalty} \ge 0$: 0 penalty (Safe loyalty zone).
1. **Water & Housing ($S_{\text{water}}$ - Heavily Weighted Over Distance)**:
   - Fresh Water (`plot:IsFreshWater()` - River, Lake, Oasis): **+32 pts** (Housing 5).
   - Coastal Land (`plot:IsCoastalLand()`): **+14 pts** (Housing 3).
   - No Water: **-25 pts penalty** (Housing 2 crisis - severe population growth stall).
   - *Mathematical Design Note*: The 57-point gap between Fresh Water (+32) and No Water (-25) ensures that distance bonuses (+15) can NEVER overpower the presence of fresh water.
2. **City Center Tile Base ($S_{\text{base}}$)**:
   - Plains Hills: **+12 pts** (Free 2 Food / 2 Production tile base + 3 combat defense strength).
   - Other Hills: **+5 pts** (+3 combat defense strength).
   - Luxury Resource on tile: **+8 pts** (Immediate amenity hookup, immune to pillaging).
   - Strategic Resource on tile: **+6 pts** (Immediate resource accumulation once researched).
3. **Ring 1 Workable Yields ($S_{\text{ring1}}$ - 6 adjacent tiles with Existing City Cannibalization Protection)**:
   - For subsequent cities (Cities 2, 3, 4...), tiles overlapping with existing friendly cities are discounted to prevent cannibalizing the empire:
     - In Ring 1 of an existing city (Civ 6 Engine Lock): **-8 pts**, yields zeroed.
     - Owned by an existing city or contains a built/pinned district: **-4 pts**, yields zeroed.
     - In Ring 2 of an existing city (contested border): **-2 pts**, yields discounted (Food $\times 0.7$, Prod $\times 0.8$).
     - Fresh, uncontested workable land: **+2 pts expansion bonus per tile**, plus full yields:
       - Food: $\ge 3$ Food: **+5 pts** (Fast growth to population 2), otherwise $\text{Food} \times 1.5$.
       - Production: $\ge 2$ Production: **+6 pts** (Accelerated first unit/monument), otherwise $\text{Prod} \times 2.0$.
       - Science / Culture: $\times 2.5$ pts per yield.
       - Faith: $\times 2.0$ pts per yield.
       - Gold: $\times 0.5$ pts per yield.
       - Luxury Resource: **+7 pts**, Strategic Resource: **+5 pts**, Bonus Resource: **+3 pts**.
       - Choppable Feature (Woods, Rainforest, Marsh, Stone): **+2 pts**.
       - Geothermal Fissure / Reef: **+4 pts** (Campus setup), Mountain: **+2.5 pts**.
4. **Ring 2 Workable Yields ($S_{\text{ring2}}$ - 12 tiles in radius 2 with Cannibalization Protection)**:
   - Existing city Ring 1 overlap: **-4 pts**, Ring 2 overlap: **-2 pts** (yields discounted).
   - Owned by existing city or has built/pinned district: **-2 pts**.
   - Fresh tiles: Food $\times 0.8$, Prod $\times 1.0$, Luxury: **+4 pts**, Strategic: **+3 pts**, Natural Wonder: **+10 pts**.
   - **Empire Expansion & Cannibalization Balance**:
     - $\ge 8$ fresh tiles: **+15 pts Empire Expansion Bonus** ("พื้นที่ขยายอาณาจักรใหม่").
     - $\ge 5$ cannibalized tiles: **-3 pts penalty per overlapping tile** ("ทับซ้อนพื้นที่เมืองเดิม").
5. **Movement Distance Penalty ($P_{\text{distance}}$)**:
   - 0 moves (settle in place): **0 penalty**.
   - 1 hex away: **-2 pts**, 2 hexes: **-5 pts**, 3 hexes: **-9 pts**, $> 3$ hexes: $-9 - ((\text{dist} - 3) \times 4)$ pts.
6. **Firaxis Grand Strategic AI Bonus ($B_{\text{AI}}$)**:
   - If Firaxis's `pGrandAI:GetSettlementRecommendations` also chose this tile: **+10 pts**.
7. **Distance to Existing Friendly Cities ($S_{\text{city\_dist}}$ - Subsequent Settlers)**:
   - Only evaluated when player already owns $\ge 1$ cities.
   - $\text{dist} < 4$: Cut off immediately (Civ 6 game rule: minimum 3 tiles between city centers).
   - $\text{dist} == 5$: **+18 pts** (**Golden Distance / ระยะทองคำ**: Both cities enjoy full 18-tile uninvaded inner cores with zero overlap, full 6-tile AoE coverage from Factory/Power Plant/Colosseum, shared district triangle synergies, and zero border friction).
   - $\text{dist} \in \{4, 6\}$: **+10 pts** ($\text{dist} = 4$ compact empire / $\text{dist} = 6$ standard workable distance).
   - $\text{dist} \ge 7$: **-10 pts** (Too far from empire, misses district synergy and AoE power).
   - **New Resource Claiming Exception with Loyalty Safeguard**: If Rings 1–3 contain an unowned Luxury or Strategic resource that the empire does not possess yet, AND $\text{Loyalty} \ge -5$, add **+20 pts** compensation (turning distant $-10$ into net $+10$ to actively claim vital unowned resources). If $\text{Loyalty} < -5$, the +20 bonus is withheld to prevent settling high-risk border locations.

#### Pin Placement:
- Top 3 scored plots receive Map Pins with icon `ICON_DISTRICT_CITY_CENTER` and labels:
  - `#1 ตั้งเมือง [XX คะแนน]`
  - `#2 ตั้งเมือง [XX คะแนน]`
  - `#3 ตั้งเมือง [XX คะแนน]`

#### 3.2 City District Boost Optimizer (`OptimizeCityDistricts`)

When a city is settled, or when `Shift + A` is pressed, the optimizer performs a sequenced constraint-satisfaction assignment across all 16 specialty districts:

```
[City Detected: Capital / Secondary] 
      │
      ├─► Clear Previous Auto-Pins for This City
      │
      ├─► Step A: Place Aqueduct (City Center + River/Lake/Mountain)
      ├─► Step B: Place Dam (Floodplains with >= 2 river edges)
      ├─► Step C: Place Canal (Flat land connecting water bodies / city center)
      ├─► Step D: Place Industrial Zone (Adjacent to Aqueduct + Dam + Canal + Strategics + Mines)
      ├─► Step E: Place Harbor (Coast water + City Center / Sea resources)
      ├─► Step F: Place Commercial Hub (River + Harbor + District cluster)
      ├─► Step G: Place Campus (Mountains + Geothermal + Reefs + Rainforests)
      ├─► Step H: Place Holy Site (Mountains + Natural Wonders + Woods)
      ├─► Step I: Place Entertainment Complex (Amenity hub + neighbor for Theater Square)
      ├─► Step J: Place Theater Square (+2 from Entertainment Complex + Wonders + Clusters)
      ├─► Step K: Place Government Plaza (1/empire, Capital priority, central +1 boost hub)
      ├─► Step L: Place Diplomatic Quarter (1/empire, Envoy boost + district cluster)
      ├─► Step M: Place Encampment (Strictly >= 2 hexes from City Center, Hills/defense)
      ├─► Step N: Place Neighborhood (Highest Appeal >= 2, up to +6 Housing)
      ├─► Step O: Place Aerodrome (Flat land, outer ring, 4 air slots)
      └─► Step P: Place Spaceport (Flat land, outer ring, Science Victory projects)
```

1. **City Tagging & Pin Naming Convention**:
   - Each pin name is formatted with the City Name at the front followed by its build priority rank:
     `[CityName] #Rank DistrictName (Yield/Effect)`
     (e.g., `[Washington] #1 Campus (+4 [ICON_Science] Sci)` or `[Washington] #2 Aqueduct (Housing)`).
   - "เมืองรอง" or "เมืองหลัก" prefixes are eliminated to maintain concise, readable map labels.
2. **Preservation of Manual Pins**:
   - Any genuine pin manually placed by the player is respected and will never be overwritten.
3. **Dam 1-per-River System, River Floodplain & Proximity Optimization (`IsDamAlreadyOnRiver`, `IsValidRiverFloodplainForDam`)**:
   - Civ 6 Gathering Storm enforces that only 1 Dam can exist per river system across the entire map.
   - Dam placement requires valid River Floodplain terrain (`FEATURE_FLOODPLAINS_GRASSLAND`, `FEATURE_FLOODPLAINS_PLAINS`, `FEATURE_FLOODPLAINS`) with $\ge 2$ river edges.
   - **Coastal Floodplains Strictly Forbidden**: Checks `TerrainManager.GetCoastalLowlandType(plot) ~= -1` to reject coastal lowlands vulnerable to sea-level flooding, ensuring Dams are only placed on true river floodplains.
   - **Scored Proximity & Synergy Selection**: Instead of naively selecting the first arbitrary floodplain (which previously led to Dams being placed at distance 3 directly in another city's face), candidate Dam plots are scored strategically:
     - Ring 1 preference (+50 pts) > Ring 2 (+25 pts) >> Ring 3 (-20 pts).
     - Ring 1 Aqueduct synergy (+35 pts): Validates if the Dam can be adjacent to a viable Aqueduct in Ring 1, enabling the massive Industrial Zone + Aqueduct + Dam triangle (+4 to +5 Prod).
     - Buildable plot expansion space (+4 pts per adjacent workable hex).
     - **Anti-Sticking & Border Rejections (Evaluated against OTHER cities strictly; this city's own center is completely excluded)**:
       - Distance to ANY other city $< 2$: Strictly rejected (Civ 6 Engine Lock on neighbor cities; this city's own Ring 1 is never rejected and gains the full +50 pts).
       - Distance to ANY other city $\le 2$ when distance from this city $\ge 2$: Strictly rejected (prevents placing Dams in another city's Ring 2).
       - Closer to another city than to this city ($\text{distToOther} < \text{distFromCity}$): Strictly rejected.
       - Outer Ring 3 Dam bordering another city ($\text{distFromCity} == 3$ and $\text{distToOther} \le 3$): Strictly rejected.
       - If no candidate achieves a positive score, Dam placement is skipped for this city to prevent corrupting the river system.
4. **Resource Tile Restrictions & Harvest Techs (`HasForbiddenResourceForDistrict`)**:
   - **Luxury Resources**: Districts are never placed on Luxury resources (the Civ 6 engine completely forbids crushing luxuries).
   - **Strategic Resources**: Districts are blocked from placing on Strategic resources once revealed by researched technologies (`pPlayer:GetResources():IsResourceVisible(rInfo.Hash)`). Unrevealed strategics remain eligible until discovered.
   - **Bonus Resources**: Districts may crush Bonus resources (Stone, Wheat, Cattle, Sheep, Deer, Rice, Bananas, Copper, Maize), but ONLY IF the player has researched the requisite harvest technology (`GameInfo.Resource_Harvests` / `pPlayer:GetTechs():HasTech`). If the tech is unresearched, the tile is strictly blocked.
5. **Canal Valid Geometry & 60-Degree Turn Rule (`IsValidCanalPosition`)**:
   - Must be flat land connecting 2 water bodies or 1 water body + 1 City Center.
   - Strictly enforces that the two connected endpoints are NOT adjacent to each other ($\text{distance} \ge 2$). If the endpoints were adjacent, they would form a $60^\circ$ sharp bend which the Civ 6 engine forbids.
6. **Workable 3-Ring Radius**:
   - Districts are strictly confined to plots with $1 \le \text{Distance} \le 3$ from the City Center.
7. **Civilization-Specific Unique Districts (UDs)**:
   - Checks player civilization traits to substitute unique districts automatically:
     - `DISTRICT_INDUSTRIAL_ZONE` $\to$ `DISTRICT_HANSA` (Germany) / `DISTRICT_OPPIDUM` (Gaul)
     - `DISTRICT_CAMPUS` $\to$ `DISTRICT_SEOWON` (Korea) / `DISTRICT_OBSERVATORY` (Maya)
     - `DISTRICT_COMMERCIAL_HUB` $\to$ `DISTRICT_SUGUBA` (Mali)
     - `DISTRICT_AQUEDUCT` $\to$ `DISTRICT_BATH` (Rome)
     - `DISTRICT_HOLY_SITE` $\to$ `DISTRICT_LAVRA` (Russia)
     - `DISTRICT_HARBOR` $\to$ `DISTRICT_ROYAL_NAVY_DOCKYARD` (England) / `DISTRICT_COTHON` (Phoenicia)
     - `DISTRICT_THEATER` $\to$ `DISTRICT_ACROPOLIS` (Greece)
     - `DISTRICT_ENCAMPMENT` $\to$ `DISTRICT_THANH` (Vietnam) / `DISTRICT_IKANDA` (Zulu)
     - `DISTRICT_NEIGHBORHOOD` $\to$ `DISTRICT_MBANZA` (Kongo)
     - `DISTRICT_ENTERTAINMENT_COMPLEX` $\to$ `DISTRICT_STREET_CARNIVAL` (Brazil)
   - **Special Civ Placement Rules**:
     - *Korea (Seowon)*: Must be placed on Hills and isolated from City Center and all other districts ($\text{dist} \ge 2$, 0 adjacent districts) to avoid the -1 Science penalty. `IsPlotAvailable` blocks future districts from adjacent hexes.
     - *Kongo (Mbanza)*: Must be placed on Woods or Rainforest only, ignoring Appeal (+5 Housing, +2 Food, +4 Gold).
     - *Vietnam (Thành & all land specialty districts)*: Must be placed on Woods, Rainforest, or Marsh only (`IsValidVietnamFeature`).
     - *Gaul (Oppidum)*: Must not be placed adjacent to the City Center ($\text{dist} \ge 2$).
8. **Population Cap & Build Priority Ranking (`CalculateDistrictPriority`)**:
   - **Non-Specialty Districts**: Aqueduct, Bath, Dam, Canal, Neighborhood, Mbanza, Spaceport, Government Plaza, and Diplomatic Quarter do NOT consume population slots (built for free / "ไม่จำกัด Pop").
   - **Specialty Districts**: Aerodrome, Campus, Industrial Zone, Commercial Hub, Harbor, Holy Site, Theater Square, Encampment, Entertainment Complex strictly count toward the population limit (Pop 1 for 1st, Pop 4 for 2nd, Pop 7 for 3rd, etc., with Germany receiving +1 free district).
   - All planned districts are sorted into strategic build priority (`#1`, `#2`, `#3`...) based on game era importance, adjacency yield strength, and specialty requirements.
9. **Recognition of Existing Built / In-Progress Districts & Manual Pins**:
   - Scans existing constructed districts and under-construction foundations via `pCity:GetDistricts()` and `plot:GetDistrictType() ~= -1`, as well as player-placed manual district pins (`HasManualPinAtPlot`).
   - **Duplicate Elimination**: If the city already possesses a district (e.g. Encampment, Campus, Industrial Zone), the optimizer immediately skips planning a redundant copy (`not CityHasDistrict(...)`), preventing duplicate pins when the original pin was auto-removed upon building foundation placement.
   - **Infrastructure Adjacency Integration**: Already constructed districts are registered as `effective<District>Plot`. Remaining unbuilt districts calculate their adjacency bonuses seamlessly from these existing buildings (e.g., an unbuilt Industrial Zone gains +2 from an already constructed Aqueduct/Dam/Canal; an unbuilt Commercial Hub gains +2 from an existing Harbor; an unbuilt Theater Square gains +2 from an existing Entertainment Complex; all districts receive the standard +0.5 adjacent district bonus).
   - **Dynamic Population Cap Offset**: The count of existing specialty districts (`existingSpecialtyCount`) is factored into population requirement thresholds (`PopReq`) so subsequent specialty districts correctly demand Pop 4, Pop 7, Pop 10, etc.
   - **HUD UI Dual Status Display**: The District Planning HUD panel lists planned districts first in priority order (`#1`, `#2`...), followed by existing completed districts (`[สร้างแล้ว] <DistrictName> • เสร็จแล้วที่ (X, Y)`). The panel header displays a combined overview: `ผังเขต [ชื่อเมือง] (สร้างแล้ว X • รอสร้าง Y):`.
10. **Collision Avoidance**:
   - Tracks assigned plots so that no two districts are ever planned on the same hex.
11. **Detailed Map Tacks Yield Broadcast**:
   - Upon creating the pins, `Network.BroadcastPlayerInfo()` and `LuaEvents.DMT_MapPinAdded` are triggered, causing DMT to immediately compute and render the exact yield badges over the pins on screen!
12. **Global Engine-Lock & Zero-Tolerance Core Encroachment (`isInvadingOtherCityCore`)**:
   - **Civ 6 Engine Constraint**: The 6 core tiles directly adjacent to ANY City Center on the entire map (Ring 1, $\text{distance} \le 1$) are permanently locked to that city by the game engine and can **never** be swapped across cities. Ring 2 ($\text{distance} \le 2$) represents a city's essential core workable tiles.
   - **Zero-Tolerance Core Encroachment Rule**: The optimizer caches all cities across the world (`allCitiesOnMap`). Any tile where $\text{Map.GetPlotDistance}(px, py, oc.X, oc.Y) \le 2$ (within 2 tiles of ANY other city center) is strictly forbidden for ALL districts of this city (`isInvadingOtherCityCore = true`), added to `occupiedPlots`, and excluded from candidates. This completely eliminates the bug where specialty districts (Campus, Holy Site, Commercial Hub, Industrial Zone, Encampment, etc.) get placed next to another city.
13. **Capital Priority & Anti-Starvation Protection (`isCapitalProtected` & Execution Ordering)**:
   - **Capital First Execution Order**: Whenever a new secondary city is settled (`Events.CityAddedToMap`), or when a secondary city runs optimization while the Capital has no planned districts yet, the system automatically prioritizes and executes `OptimizeCityDistricts` for the Capital first before allowing the secondary city to plan.
   - **Capital Inner Ring Reservation (Rings 1 & 2 Lock)**: Secondary cities are strictly forbidden from planning districts on any tile within Rings 1 and 2 of the Capital ($\text{Map.GetPlotDistance}(px, py, \text{capitalX}, \text{capitalY}) \le 2$). These prime tiles are reserved exclusively for the Capital to ensure it never gets starved of districts.
   - **Cross-City Pin Reservation**: Secondary cities inspect existing auto-pins and map tacks. If a plot is already tagged or pinned for another city (`isClaimedByOtherCity`), secondary cities cannot steal it and must choose from remaining open tiles.
   - **Safe City-Scoped Pin Deletion**: `ClearAutoDistrictsForCity` strictly filters by `autoInfo.CityX == cityX and autoInfo.CityY == cityY` and city name prefix instead of performing a blind radial wipe. When the Capital is cleared or re-optimized, it actively reclaims any tiles in its Rings 1 & 2 that were previously misplaced.
   - **Strict Aqueduct Adjacency**: Aqueduct placement explicitly requires $\text{Map.GetPlotDistance}(cityX, cityY, px, py) == 1$, preventing edge-case selection of distant tiles.
   - **Sorted Re-planning Queue**: In `ValidateAndRefreshAutoPins`, cities scheduled for dynamic re-planning are sorted so that the Capital is always processed before secondary cities.
14. **Anti-Encroachment, Tile Ownership & Frontier-Facing Defense (`isOwnedByAnotherCity`, `isContestedBorder`, Encampment)**:
   - **Tile Ownership Integrity**: Uses `GetPlotOwningCity(plot, playerID)` to verify tile possession. If a plot is already owned by another friendly city (`isOwnedByAnotherCity`), it is strictly excluded from candidate plots, preventing one city from seizing another city's claimed tiles.
   - **Sphere of Influence Rule**: A city may never place any district on a hex that is closer to another city than to itself ($\text{minDistToAnyCity} < \text{distFromCity}$).
   - **Contested Border Rejection**: Outer perimeter tiles at Ring 3 that are within 3 tiles of another city ($\text{distFromCity} == 3 \text{ and } \text{minDistToAnyCity} \le 3$) are excluded (`isContestedBorder = true`), preventing placing districts on contested midline borders.
   - **Encampment Frontier-Facing Defense**: Encampments strictly require $\text{minOtherCityDist} \ge 3$ (never on the border of friendly cities) and gain a +3 score bonus when oriented outward into the wild frontier away from friendly cities ($\text{minOtherCityDist} \ge 4$).

### 3.3 Turn-by-Turn Dynamic Border & District Validation (`ValidateAndRefreshAutoPins`)

- **Execution Triggers**:
  - `Events.LocalPlayerTurnBegin`: At the beginning of each turn.
  - `Events.PlayerTurnActivated`: When player controls become active.
  - `Events.LoadGameViewStateDone`: Upon loading a savegame.
- **Validation Checks**:
  1. **Foreign Border Expansion / Conquest**: If an opponent civ claims a tile previously reserved for a district (`plot:IsOwned() and plot:GetOwner() ~= playerID`), the tack is immediately deleted and the owning city is flagged for automatic re-planning.
  2. **District Already Built**: If a district or wonder is constructed on that plot (`plot:GetDistrictType() ~= -1 or plot:IsCity()`), the tack is cleared cleanly.
  3. **City Lost or Razed**: If the owning city no longer belongs to the player, all its auto-pins are deleted.
- **Automatic Re-planning**:
  - For any city whose district was blocked or lost to border shifts, `OptimizeCityDistricts` runs automatically to re-route remaining districts to new optimal plots.

## 6. Stability, Memory Safety & Engine Crash Prevention (`0xffffffff` Protection)

Civilization VI's Lua API operates as a thin binding layer over an underlying C++ simulation and UI engine. Several patterns that are standard in pure Lua will cause instant C++ engine crashes (`EXCEPTION_ACCESS_VIOLATION Error reading address 0xffffffff` / `(uint32_t)-1`). The following memory safety patterns are strictly enforced across all files:

### 6.1 Deferred Map Pin Deletion Outside Iteration Loops
* **The Danger**:
  Calling `playerCfg:DeleteMapPin(...)` while actively traversing pins with `for pinID, pin in pairs(allPins)` mutates and reallocates the underlying C++ vector/map in memory. The iterator pointer becomes dangling, and the subsequent loop step accesses invalid memory at `0xffffffff`, terminating the game process instantly.
  Furthermore, `pinID` from `pairs(allPins)` is the 1-based Lua table array index (`1`, `2`, `3`...), NOT the unique pin ID expected by the C++ engine (`pin:GetID()`).
* **The Required Pattern**:
  Always buffer pins marked for removal into a temporary table (`pinsToDelete`) along with the true C++ ID (`pin:GetID()`), complete the iteration loop without touching the engine container, and then iterate through the buffer to delete pins outside the loop. Deduplicate IDs using a hash set (`deletedIDs`) and wrap all C++ engine calls in `pcall()`:
  ```lua
  local allPins = playerCfg:GetMapPins();
  local pinsToDelete = {};
  if allPins ~= nil then
      for _, pin in pairs(allPins) do
          if pin ~= nil then
              -- evaluation logic...
              if bShouldDelete then
                  table.insert(pinsToDelete, { ID = pin:GetID(), Pin = pin, Key = key });
              end
          end
      end
  end

  local deletedIDs = {};
  for _, item in ipairs(pinsToDelete) do
      if item.ID ~= nil and not deletedIDs[item.ID] then
          deletedIDs[item.ID] = true;
          pcall(function() LuaEvents.DMT_MapPinRemoved(item.Pin); end);
          pcall(function() playerCfg:DeleteMapPin(item.ID); end);
      end
  end
  ```

### 6.2 Event Debouncing & City Foundation Race Condition Mitigation
* **The Danger**:
  When a city is settled, the Civ 6 engine fires `Events.CityAddedToMap`, `Events.CityInitialized`, and `Events.CityNameChanged` back-to-back within the exact same frame. If all three events invoke `OptimizeCityDistricts`, it triggers rapid, redundant pin creation and deletion cycles on uncommitted C++ map data, causing race conditions and memory faults.
* **The Solution**:
  1. Only subscribe to `Events.CityAddedToMap` for city creation; eliminate redundant listeners for `CityInitialized` and `CityNameChanged`.
  2. Implement an in-memory turn debounce map: `m_LastOptimizedCities[cityKey] = currentTurn`.
  3. When `OptimizeCityDistricts` is called without `bForce = true`, it verifies whether that city has already been optimized during the current turn. Redundant invocations are cleanly skipped.
  4. Manual triggers (e.g. user pressing `Shift + A`, UI button clicks, or automatic turn validation re-planning) pass `bForce = true` to guarantee immediate execution when requested.

### 6.3 Safe Loyalty Lens Queries (`pcall` Protection)
* **The Danger**:
  Directly invoking `Map.GetContinentPlotsLoyalty()` during turn transitions or before the Gathering Storm lens system has finished generating continent tables can result in unhandled C++ access violations.
* **The Solution**:
  Wrap queries in a protected call:
  ```lua
  local pcallOk, loyaltyTable = pcall(function() return Map.GetContinentPlotsLoyalty(); end);
  if pcallOk and loyaltyTable ~= nil then
      local val = loyaltyTable[plot:GetIndex()];
      -- ...
  end
  ```

## 7. UI Layout, Hitbox & Mouse Input Architecture

### 7.1 Preventing Screen-Wide Invisible Mouse Blockers (Top-Right Corner & Production Panel Protection)
* **The Root Causes**:
  1. **Z-Order Inversion from `AdditionalUserInterfaces`**:
     `dmt_yieldcalculator.xml` is injected via `<AddUserInterfaces>` into `<Container ID="AdditionalUserInterfaces"/>` at line 133 of `InGame.xml`. This sits significantly higher in Z-order than `TopPanel` (line 48: Pause menu cog, Civilopedia, Turns, Clock at top-right), `ProductionPanel` (line 57: Unit production list on right), and `DiplomacyRibbon` (line 76: Leader portraits).
  2. **Root Context Mouse Swallowing**:
     The root `<Context>` defaults to full-screen (1920x1080). If `<Context>` lacks `ConsumeMouse="0"`, ForgeUI treats the invisible background of the context as an active hit-test target at Z-order 133, swallowing mouse clicks before they can reach `TopPanel` or `ProductionPanel`.
  3. **Modal Input Flag (`bModal = true`) Hijacking**:
     Registering `ContextPtr:SetInputHandler(handler, true)` with `true` designates the context as MODAL in Civ 6's ForgeUI engine. A modal context at Z-order 133 traps global input and prevents clicks on underlying non-modal HUD elements (such as the top-right Menu cog button and Civilopedia).
  4. **Unclipped Label Text Overflow & Missing ScrollBar**:
     Right-anchored labels without `Align="Right"` or `TruncateWidth` can expand parent Grids horizontally across the screen. Additionally, `<ScrollPanel AutoScrollBar="1">` without an explicit `<ScrollBar>` child can cause ForgeUI to synthesize unconstrained hitboxes.
* **The Solution**:
  1. **Enforce `ConsumeMouse="0"` on Root Context**:
     `<Context ConsumeMouse="0">` explicitly instructs ForgeUI to pass mouse clicks through on all empty screen areas to underlying layers (`TopPanel`, `DiplomacyRibbon`, `ProductionPanel`, and 3D World Map).
  2. **Enforce `ConsumeMouse="1"` on Floating Panels Only**:
     Set `ConsumeMouse="1"` strictly on `<Grid ID="CityDistrictPlanPanel">` (380px, center-top) and `<Grid ID="SettlerRecommendationPanel">` (340px, left-top). This guarantees clicks inside the panels don't click-through to the 3D map, while leaving the entire right screen and top-right corner 100% transparent.
  3. **Register Input Handlers as Non-Modal (`bModal = false`)**:
     Pass `false` in `ContextPtr:SetInputHandler(OnSmartPlannerInputHandler, false)` and `mappinmanager_dmt.lua`. Both continue to intercept `ESC` and `Shift + A` key events without ever locking ForgeUI's modal focus.
  4. **ScrollPanel with Internal ScrollBar**:
     Wrap `DistrictListStack` inside `<ScrollPanel ID="DistrictScrollPanel" Size="360,220" Vertical="1" AutoScrollBar="1">` containing an internal `<ScrollBar Style="Slider_Blue" Anchor="R,C" AnchorSide="I,I" Offset="0,0"/>`.
  5. **Comprehensive Auto-Dismissal**:
     Automatically hides panels on:
     - City deselection (`Events.CitySelectionChanged`)
     - Production panel opening (`LuaEvents.ProductionPanel_Open`, `LuaEvents.CityPanel_ProductionOpen`)
     - Turn end (`Events.LocalPlayerTurnEnd`)
     - Escape key (`Keys.VK_ESCAPE`)
     - Panel close button (`[X]`) clicks
